CSIR-UGC NET Life Sciences High-Yield Vault

Unit-wise curated summary notes, formulas, and high-frequency Part-C problem areas.

Unit 1: Molecules & Their Interaction (Biochemistry) Toggle Notes ▾

• Protein Conformation: Ramachandran plot angles ($\\phi, \\psi$); Glycine has greatest conformational flexibility, Proline is constrained.

• Enzyme Kinetics: $V = \\frac{V_{max}[S]}{K_m + [S]}$; Competitive inhibition (increases apparent $K_m$, $V_{max}$ unchanged); Uncompetitive inhibition (decreases both $V_{max}$ and $K_m$).

• Bioenergetics: Standard free energy change $\\Delta G^\circ = -RT \\ln K_{eq}$; coupled reactions drive thermodynamically unfavorable pathways.

Unit 2: Cellular Organization & Membrane Dynamics Toggle Notes ▾

• Membrane Structure: Lipid rafts enriched in cholesterol and sphingolipids; FRAP measures lateral diffusion coefficient ($D$).

• Protein Sorting: Signal recognition particle (SRP) pathways; KDEL retrieval sequences for ER-resident proteins; NLS targeting to nucleus via importins.

• Cell Cycle: Cyclin-CDK checkpoints (CDK1/Cyclin B for G2-M; p53-p21 axis arresting damaged cells at G1-S).

Unit 4: Cell Communication & Immunology Toggle Notes ▾

• Signaling Pathways: GPCR (G$\\alpha_s$ activates adenylate cyclase; G$\\alpha_q$ activates PLC-$\\beta$ generating IP3 & DAG); Receptor Tyrosine Kinases (RTK $\\rightarrow$ Ras-MAPK).

• Apoptosis: Intrinsic pathway (Bcl-2 family, Cytochrome c release from mitochondria, Apaf-1 apoptosome, Caspase-9/3) vs. Extrinsic (FasL / TNF-$\\alpha$, Caspase-8).

• Immunology: MHC Class I presents endogenous peptides to CD8+ T cells; MHC Class II presents exogenous peptides to CD4+ T cells.

Unit 13: Methods in Biology (Critical for Part C) Toggle Notes ▾

• Biophysical Methods: Circular Dichroism (CD) detects $\\alpha$-helices (208 & 222 nm) and $\\beta$-sheets (218 nm); FRET measures molecular distance between 1–10 nm ($E = \\frac{R_0^6}{R_0^6 + r^6}$).

• Molecular Biology: CRISPR/Cas9 (sgRNA + PAM sequence NGG); RT-qPCR relative quantification via $\\Delta\\Delta C_t$ method.

• Separation Techniques: Size-exclusion chromatography (large proteins elute first); SDS-PAGE separates strictly based on molecular mass.
